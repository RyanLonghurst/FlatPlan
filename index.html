Flatplan

Plan your flat before you move in

Upload a floor plan, trace the rooms over it, then try furniture layouts to scale and walk through them in 3D. Everything stays in your browser.

  1. Set the scaleDraw a line along a room the plan gives a size for and type the size in.
  2. Trace the roomsDrag a box over each room. L-shaped rooms take two boxes.
  3. Add doors and windowsTap a wall to place them, then flip them to open the right way.
  4. Furnish and walk throughDrag furniture in, check it fits, save layouts and tour it in 3D.

Your plans
